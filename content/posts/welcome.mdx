## Why a portal like this

A researcher portal does a few simple jobs well: a research overview, a
publication list, and a place to write. Everything here is plain text first.

- One file per post under `content/posts`.
- Frontmatter for title, date, and excerpt.
- GitHub-flavoured Markdown, including tables.
